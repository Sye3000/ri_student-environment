ABOUT RI STUDENT ENVIRONMENT

Connecting Students. Building Community.

RI Student Environment is a student-focused platform designed to connect students across campuses, create opportunities for participation, and build a stronger student community.

WHO WE ARE

More Than a Student Platform

RI Student Environment brings students together in one digital environment where they can discover activities, participate in programs, attend events, and engage with other students.

Our goal is to make student life more connected, accessible, and collaborative by creating a space where students can share ideas, ask questions, discover opportunities, and take part in campus activities.

About RI Student Environment

Building a connected student community

RI Student Environment is a proposed student-focused digital environment designed to connect students, provide access to opportunities and encourage participation across the student community.

Who We Are

RI Student Environment provides a central digital space where students can discover programmes, events, community activities and student leadership information.

The platform is designed around student engagement, participation and access to useful information.

Our Purpose

The purpose of RI Student Environment is to make student-related information easier to discover and provide a structured environment for students to participate and connect.

Our Objectives

  • Improve access to student information.
  • Promote student programmes and opportunities.
  • Provide information about student events.
  • Support student leadership visibility.
  • Encourage student community participation.
  • Create a foundation for future digital student services.

About Student Representation

Student representation provides students with a structured way to communicate their views, concerns and ideas within the student community.

The RI Student Environment provides a digital space where information about student leadership and representation can be made easier to access.

What the SRC Can Support

Student Representation

Provide a channel through which student concerns, ideas and interests can be represented.

Student Communication

Help students access relevant information and communicate with student representatives.

Student Activities

Support awareness and participation in student activities and initiatives.

Encourage students to participate in discussions and community initiatives.

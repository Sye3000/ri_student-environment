Creating Positive Change

Our Impact

RI Student Environment aims to create a more connected student community by improving access to information, opportunities and participation.

Our Intended Impact

The platform is designed to support students by bringing important student-focused information, opportunities and community activities together in one digital environment.

By creating a central student environment, RI Student Environment aims to make it easier for students to discover opportunities, connect with one another and participate in campus life.

Areas of Impact

01

Better Access to Information

Students can have a central place to discover information about programmes, events, activities and student opportunities.

02

Stronger Student Connection

The platform aims to make it easier for students to connect with other students and participate in community activities.

03

Increased Participation

Students can discover opportunities that may encourage greater involvement in programmes, events and student activities.

04

Student Leadership Visibility

The SRC section provides a dedicated area for student leadership and representation information.

05

Community Engagement

The community environment is intended to encourage communication, collaboration and constructive student interaction.

06

Future Student Opportunities

Future development can expand access to additional student services, programmes, events and digital opportunities.

Benefits for Students

RI Student Environment is intended to provide practical benefits that improve the overall student experience.

  • Easier access to student information.
  • Greater awareness of programmes and events.
  • More opportunities to connect with other students.
  • Improved visibility of student leadership.
  • More opportunities for community participation.

Measuring Future Impact

Once the platform is fully implemented, its impact can be evaluated using measurable indicators. These indicators can help determine whether the platform is achieving its intended objectives.

01

Registered Students

Measure the number of students who register and actively use the platform.

02

Programme Participation

Track student participation in programmes and development opportunities.

03

Event Participation

Measure attendance and engagement across student events and activities.

04

Community Engagement

Monitor student interaction, collaboration and participation within the community.

05

Student Feedback

Use student feedback to identify improvements and measure satisfaction with the platform.

Building for the Future

RI Student Environment is designed with future development in mind. Additional functionality can be introduced to improve communication, participation, student services and access to opportunities.

These improvements can be evaluated as the platform develops through later project phases, allowing the environment to grow according to student needs.

Be Part of the Impact

Students can contribute to a stronger student environment by participating in programmes, events and community activities.

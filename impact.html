Creating Positive Change

Our Impact

RI Student Environment aims to create a more connected student community by improving access to information, opportunities and participation.

Our Intended Impact

The platform is designed to support students by bringing important student-focused information and opportunities together in one digital environment.

Areas of Impact

Better Access to Information

Students can have a central place to discover information about programmes, events, activities and student opportunities.

Stronger Student Connection

The platform aims to make it easier for students to connect with other students and participate in community activities.

Increased Participation

Students can discover opportunities that may encourage greater involvement in programmes, events and student activities.

Student Leadership Visibility

The SRC section provides a dedicated area for student leadership and representation information.

Community Engagement

The community environment is intended to encourage communication, collaboration and constructive student interaction.

Benefits for Students

  • Easier access to student information.
  • Greater awareness of programmes and events.
  • More opportunities to connect with other students.
  • Improved visibility of student leadership.
  • Opportunities for community participation.

Measuring Future Impact

Once the platform is fully implemented, its impact could be evaluated using measurable indicators.

  • Number of registered students.
  • Number of programme participants.
  • Event participation.
  • Community engagement.
  • Student feedback.

Building for the Future

Future development can introduce additional functionality that improves communication, participation and access to student services.

These improvements can be evaluated as the platform develops through later project phases.

Be Part of the Impact

Students can contribute to a stronger student environment by participating in programmes, events and community activities.

Student Connection

Welcome to the Community

Connect with students, share ideas, ask questions and discover opportunities to participate in the student community.

A Place to Connect

The RI Student Environment community is designed to provide students with a structured space for communication, collaboration and information sharing.

Students can use the platform to discover discussions, activities and opportunities that may be relevant to their interests and campus life.

Community Areas

Student Discussions

Share ideas, discuss student experiences and participate in constructive conversations.

Questions and Answers

Ask questions and share useful information with other members of the student community.

Study and Learning

Connect with students who are interested in learning, collaboration and academic support.

Student Activities

Discover activities, programmes and events where students can participate together.

Community Guidelines

A positive student environment depends on respectful and responsible participation.

  • Treat other students with respect.
  • Keep discussions constructive and relevant.
  • Do not share harmful or inappropriate content.
  • Respect other people's privacy.
  • Report inappropriate behaviour through the appropriate channels.

How to Participate

  1. Create or access your student account.
  2. Explore community discussions.
  3. Ask questions or share useful information.
  4. Participate respectfully.
  5. Connect with other students.

Future Community Features

Interactive community functionality can be developed during later phases of the project.

  • Student discussion boards
  • Questions and answers
  • Student profiles
  • Community groups
  • Notifications

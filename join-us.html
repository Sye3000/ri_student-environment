Join the Student Environment

Become part of a student-focused environment designed to help students connect, discover opportunities and participate in their community.

Why Join?

Joining the RI Student Environment can provide students with a central place to discover information, opportunities, events and community activities.

  • Discover student programmes.
  • Find upcoming events.
  • Connect with other students.
  • Participate in community activities.
  • Access student-focused information.

How to Join

  1. Create your student account.
  2. Provide the required registration information.
  3. Confirm your details.
  4. Access the student environment.
  5. Explore programmes, events and community opportunities.

Registration Information

The registration process can collect relevant student information required to create an account.

  • Full name
  • Student number
  • Student email address
  • Campus
  • Password
